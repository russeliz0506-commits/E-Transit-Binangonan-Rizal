User guide

  1. Choose your destination and direction.
  2. Select a route option and day to view the sample timetable and stops.
  3. Check the color and passenger count in Bus status. Green means less crowded; red means near capacity.
  4. Check the fare shown in the trip summary. In this prototype, fares are placeholders and must be replaced with official fares.
  5. Use Arrival reminder to test a browser notification. Real arrival alerts require GPS permission and live location integration.

The site is free to open and does not ask users to create an account. Publishing it online requires hosting. A real-time production service will also need a secure backend, verified operator data, and privacy/safety procedures for driver and plate information.
